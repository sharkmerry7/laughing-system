About Calorie Per Dollar

The calorie per dollar metric helps you identify which menu items provide the most energy (calories) for your money. This is useful for:

  • Budget-conscious diners looking to maximize value
  • Cafe operators analyzing menu pricing
  • Students trying to get the most from their meal plans
  • Anyone interested in food economics
